Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Can I customise my package?
Absolutely. All packages serve as a base — our specialists will tailor every detail to your preferences, from accommodation style to activity choices.
What is the cancellation policy?
We offer full refunds up to 30 days before departure, 50% refund up to 14 days, and rescheduling options up to 7 days prior.
Are visas included in the package?
Visa assistance is included in all Signature and Prestige packages. Explorer package clients receive guidance and documentation support.
Do you cater for solo travellers?
Yes! We have dedicated solo traveller rates for all packages. We also organise small-group departures where you can meet like-minded explorers.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 6–8 weeks in advance for peak season travel (October–February, June–August). Last-minute options are sometimes available.
Is travel insurance mandatory?
Travel insurance is included in all packages. We strongly recommend not opting out, as it covers medical emergencies, trip cancellations, and lost luggage.
